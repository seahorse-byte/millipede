## Rubric dimensions

For each scenario ask:

1. **Constraint** — enterprise auth? teaching clarity? eval CI?
2. **Choose** — pattern that fits *this* context
3. **Avoid** — seductive copy-paste from another repo

Scenarios in the widget mirror `docs/work-alignment.md` — teaching stack vs Snyk entitlements vs eval gates.

> "I can ship quality systems on the PDF architecture *and* explain when work-repo patterns apply elsewhere."
