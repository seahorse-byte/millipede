When producers outpace consumers, **lag** grows — that is backpressure visible in metrics.

## Offsets

**KafkaOffsetRewind** — burst +5 messages, consume one-by-one, or replay from offset 0.

| Action | Dev | Prod |
|--------|-----|------|
| Reset consumer group | Reprocess test data | Dangerous — duplicate side effects |
| Scale consumers | Add llm-worker replicas | Same |

## Idempotency

Replay requires **idempotent writes** (e.g. upsert by event `id`) or you duplicate Postgres rows.
